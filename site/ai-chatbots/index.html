Questions

AI Chatbot FAQ

Do I need a website?

Yes — the chatbot lives on your website. No website yet? We can build one for you and add the chatbot at the same time.

What if the chatbot doesn't know an answer?

It tells the customer politely and takes their details, then sends the question to your staff by email so a real person can reply.

Can I change what it knows?

Yes. Email us new prices, products or policies any time and we update it. On every plan we refresh its knowledge each month.

Is my customers' information safe?

Conversations are stored securely and only used to help your customers and improve your chatbot. We never sell or share them.
