Questions

Nadine Vault FAQ

When can I download the app?

Nadine Vault for Android is launching on Google Play soon. Email us for early access and we'll let you know the moment it's live. An iPhone and computer version will follow.

Where are my files stored?

Your files are encrypted and stored in a secure, professional data centre in the European Union (Amsterdam). Only you can see them — we never sell or share your files.

How do I pay for more space?

Inside the app, choose a plan and pay with mobile money through Nadine Pay. Your extra space is ready straight away. Plans are billed per month.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Just install Nadine Vault on your new phone and sign in — all your backed-up photos, videos and files are still there.
