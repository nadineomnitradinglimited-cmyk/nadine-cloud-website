About Nadine Technologies

A pure technology company, founded in Oman.

Founded in Oman, with operations in South Africa, Kenya and Zambia, Nadine Technologies designs, builds and runs the technology businesses depend on — from artificial intelligence, software and operating systems to custom-built phones, security devices and the cloud servers they run on.
