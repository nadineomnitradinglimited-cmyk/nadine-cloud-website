Questions

Hosting FAQ

Anything else, just email us at info@nadinecloud.com.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes — you can move up to a bigger plan at any time. We only charge the prorated difference for the rest of your billing cycle.

Do you migrate my existing website for free?

Yes — free website migration is included on every hosting plan, whichever host you're moving from.

Can I run a Python, Django or Node.js app?

Our hosting plans run websites built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP — including WordPress and sites exported from website builders. Python (Django, Flask) and Node.js apps aren't included on these plans yet. Email support@nadinecloud.com and we'll recommend the right setup for your app.

Can I deploy my site from GitHub or GitLab?

Yes — and it's automatic. Open your control panel, choose Deployments and paste your GitHub repository link. Your site goes live in under a minute, and every time you push to GitHub it updates by itself. You can even create a database for your site in the same step. See how it works.

What payment methods do you accept?

Pay at checkout by mobile money (MTN, Airtel, Zamtel) or card (Visa / Mastercard), or ask us for a bank transfer. Hosting can be billed monthly, or every 6 months, 1, 2 or 3 years for a bigger discount — pick your billing period above the plans.

What happens if my site goes down?

We monitor all hosting 24/7 and target 99.9% uptime. If there's an issue, email us and we'll respond the same day.
