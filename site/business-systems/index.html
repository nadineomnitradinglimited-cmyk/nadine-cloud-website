Business Systems FAQ

Does it work if the internet goes off?

Yes. The system keeps working on your computer, and everything is copied to your online backup as soon as the internet is back.

Can you move our existing records in?

Yes. Send us your current records (Excel, another system or paper lists) and we'll help bring them into your new system during setup.

Do I need special computers?

No. Any normal Windows computer works, and the clinic phone app runs on Android phones.

Can I see it before I pay?
